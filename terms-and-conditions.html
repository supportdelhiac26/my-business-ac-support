Terms and Conditions

About the service

Delhi Appliance Care is an independent repair service for home appliances. We are not the manufacturer, and we are not an authorised or official service centre of any appliance brand. Repairs done by us are not covered by the manufacturer's warranty and may affect it.

Booking and visit

A visit is booked by phone. The technician inspects the appliance and tells you the fault and the cost of repair. A visit or inspection charge may apply even if you decide not to go ahead; you will be told this charge when you book.

Charges and payment

The cost of parts and labour is agreed with you before work begins. Payment is due when the job is finished, by the methods the technician accepts.

Parts and repair warranty

Any warranty on our repair or on parts we fit is the one written on your bill. It does not cover faults that are unrelated to the repair, or damage caused by misuse, voltage fluctuation, water or pests.

Limits

Some appliances cannot be repaired economically, or parts may no longer be available. In that case we will tell you, and only the inspection charge applies.

Brand names

Brand names and logos belong to their owners. Where a brand is named on this website it only describes the appliances we repair and does not suggest any connection with that brand.
