Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you call Delhi Appliance Care to book a repair, we note your name, phone number, address and the details of the appliance fault. This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account.

How we use it

We use these details only to arrange the visit, send the technician to the right address, and contact you about that repair or its warranty.

Sharing

Your details are given to the technician assigned to your job. We do not sell or rent customer details to anyone. We may disclose them if the law requires it.

Cookies and fonts

This website does not set its own cookies. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If visitor measurement or advertising tags are added to this site, this policy will be updated to say so.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details we hold about you by calling +91 94110 78560.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
